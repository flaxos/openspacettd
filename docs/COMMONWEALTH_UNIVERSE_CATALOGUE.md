## 1. Evidentiary Grading & Conventions

Every world and connection in this catalogue is classified according to evidence rigor:

| Grade | Classification | Description |
|:---:|---|---|
| **E** | **Explicit** | Direct novel passage explicitly describes the physical transport connection. |
| **R** | **Reference-Supported** | Secondary reference identifies the connection, or connection follows Earth-to-Phase-One structure. |
| **I** | **Inferred** | Specific geographical, sector, or journey context proposes the link, but direct permanent gate is unverified. |
| **A** | **Assumed (Game Topology)** | Editable game-design allocation connecting otherwise unplaced worlds to Big15 hubs (Rule A0). **Never canon.** |
| **U** | **Unknown / Unassigned** | No direct connection supplied in baseline graph. |

### Phase Classifications
- **P1 (Phase 1 - Core Worlds):** High-density metropolitan capitals, arcologies, heavy advanced goods demand.
- **P2 (Phase 2 - Developed / Industrial):** Secondary manufacturing, alloy smelters, chemical refineries, mixed rail.
- **P3 (Phase 3 - Frontier Colonies):** Primary resource extraction, bio-farms, mining loops, heavy freight rail.
- **P4 (Phase 4 - Wilderness / Expansion):** Unsettled worlds, survey sites, player-colonized outposts.
- **? / NA:** Phase unverified in canonical sources or not applicable (e.g. gas giant, star).

## 7. Operational Topology Rules for OpenSpaceTTD

1. **Preserve Sparse Tree Structure:** OpenSpaceTTD will not generate a dense, arbitrary mesh between worlds. The network strictly branches:
   $$\text{Sol/Earth} \longrightarrow \text{Big15 Core Hubs} \longrightarrow \text{Phase 2 Industrial Sectors} \longrightarrow \text{Phase 3 Frontier Corridors} \longrightarrow \text{Phase 4 Wilderness}$$
2. **Distinct Identities for Aliases & Spacing:**
   - Use canonical **Oaktier** (do not rename to project's colloquial "Oaktree").
   - **Democratic Republic of New Germany** normalized from DRNG.
   - **Huxley's Haven** canonical (treat "The Hive" as a cultural alias, not a second world).
   - **Valdivia** (Los Vada branch) and **Valvida** (Merredin branch) are strictly separate worlds.
3. **Modal Integrity:**
   - Under no circumstances shall `DATA` links (Vinmar) pass rolling stock.
   - Under no circumstances shall `SILFEN_ROUTE` or `ORBITAL` paths permit direct uninterrupted rail tracks without their respective intermodal transshipment depots.
