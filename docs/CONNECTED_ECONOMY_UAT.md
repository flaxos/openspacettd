# Connected economy acceptance fixture

**2026-09-23 · Automated acceptance passed; human visual UAT pending.**

Player entry point: [short UAT guide](../demo/CONNECTED-ECONOMY-UAT.md).
The v2.0 save is independent of the older all-features saves.

## Reproduce

```sh
ninja -C build
python3 scripts/generate_connected_economy.py --steps 160 --verify \
  --publish demo/OpenSpaceTTD-Connected-Economy-UAT-v2.0.sav
```

The runner needs the installed OpenGFX base set and a loopback socket for an isolated
headless game. It uses seed 11 and an empty 1024×1024 Arctic map. Runtime logs and the
cold-start save go to `build/connected-economy/`. Publishing requires every gate to
pass. The versioned evidence JSON records the binary, content and save hashes;
`.run.json.gz` contains every observation. `--steps` without `--verify` is diagnostic
and never reports acceptance or publishes a save.

Build the v3 NewGRFs with the pinned requirements in `requirements-commonwealth-grf.txt`:

```sh
NMLC=/path/to/nmlc python3 scripts/build_commonwealth_grf.py --verify
```

CMake copies both legacy v2 and new v3 packs to `build/newgrf/`, so saves can find
their exact content during reload. The legacy binaries are preserved.

## Construction and operation

The offline-only `connected_economy prepare` command refuses an occupied map or a
multiplayer session with clients. It creates four immutable logical regions within
one global map, levels an empty construction band, and starts one human company
with 100 million credits plus Materials I, Traction I and Traction II. Cargo and
factory buffers begin empty. Rail, portal, station, factory, hub, HQ, refit, order,
timetable and research operations use the normal game commands.

There are 37 trains, 28 stations and eleven processing facilities. Dedicated
corridors avoid deadlocks in the dense showcase. Five short mineral collection
services and a mixed wheat/livestock service feed export hubs; two mixed farm
freight trains feed the native food processor. City delivery services use normal
timetables. Factories retain outputs on their platforms for rail collection.
Company stockpiles remain shared within each world, as in normal gameplay.

The eleven factories cover ballast, steel, alloys, copper wiring, silicon,
signalling, polymers, propulsion, blank crystals, quantum enrichment and consumer
crystals. HQ deliveries provide construction materials and research feedstock.
The city receives real food, composites, chips and consumer crystals; its population
and houses are generated by the native town simulation.

## Repairs exercised by the fixture

- **Cargo identity:** megacity demand uses cargo labels, not numeric slots. Unrelated
  cargo, including passengers and raw ore, does not satisfy demand.
- **Consumer acceptance:** a megacity station must actually cover that town's houses.
  Its demand cargoes are accepted there. Warehouse storage and factory/native industry
  consumption do not count as city deliveries. Monthly quotas track real population.
- **Content:** industry v3 uses slots 16–28 and explicit freight flags, preserving
  native food/farm cargoes. Rail v3 adds grain/food refits and a livestock stockcar.
  Exact v2 and v3 packs remain supported; unsupported/compatible replacements fail closed.
- **Audit:** optional counters observe native industry/town production, station loss,
  accepted cargo per train, research consumption and recipe transformations. They do
  not alter the simulation and are not persistent gameplay state.

The conservation boundary includes station cargo, vehicle cargo, factory buffers
and planetary stockpiles. Native industry inputs leave this boundary on acceptance;
outputs re-enter when the engine releases production. Research is a measured sink.
Save/load drops zero-valued buffer entries, which the comparison normalizes.

## Acceptance evidence

- Every train delivers each cargo it carries repeatedly, including both farm cargoes.
- All eleven facilities produce; three consecutive fully supplied months precede the
  checkpoint, with real additional houses.
- A further 24 months preserve cargo accounting and repeated service on every route;
  city houses increase from 32 to 42 and population from 872 to 906.
- Clear growth appears after 12,288 ticks: 35 houses and 905 people, approximately
  332 seconds at the normal 27 ms tick interval. Wall time depends on game speed/load.
- Reload preserves cargo, cash, dates, stockpiles, facility progress, city state and research.
- Stopping only the food service causes starvation and stops new houses; restarting
  restores fully supplied growth. These operations run on a disposable copy.
- CST Mainline Double Straight consumes exactly 32 ballast, 18 steel and 2 wiring;
  the executed and quoted construction costs agree. Research consumes 45 delivered
  feedstock units and completes Dual-Track Throat Arrays.

Validation: **427 unit cases / 66,233 assertions; 438/438 CTests**. Both repository
linters, `git diff --check`, pinned NewGRF rebuild verification and the legacy v2
WP-11 headless economy/reload/fabrication regression pass.

## Limits

This is a deterministic logistics showcase with generous startup capital, no
breakdowns or disasters, and explicit prerequisite research. It does not prove
competitive balance, indefinite growth, or true multi-map/federated transport.
Monthly supply can fluctuate and house rebuilding can temporarily lower population.
No recurring replenishment, fake deliveries, population edits or direct growth
commands are used during warm-up or verification.

Human visual acceptance remains outstanding. A graphical inspection was unavailable
in this session because `orca-ide` was not installed. This save does not close the
remaining human UAT items for Sprints 43–50.
